# Privacy Policy for tinCK

**Effective date:** May 14, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **tinCK**. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our mobile application and related services (together, the “**Service**”). We are committed to handling your information responsibly and clearly describing our practices.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account and authentication

Depending on how you sign in, we may process:

- **Anonymous use:** Supabase can create an anonymous session so you can use parts of the app before linking an account. A unique user identifier is stored on our backend tied to that session.
- **Sign in with Apple:** Apple may share an account identifier and, if you choose, your name and email (including Apple’s private relay email). Authentication is processed through Supabase Auth.
- **Sign in with Google:** When you use Google sign-in, Google shares account-related information needed to authenticate you (for example, profile basics consistent with your Google settings). The OAuth flow uses your device browser session as required by the provider.

We store account-related fields needed for the Service, such as **user id**, **display name**, and **email** when your provider supplies it, as reflected in your profile.

### 2.2 Preferences and app content you configure

- **Watchlist / tickers:** Symbols you follow are stored so we can show relevant news, sentiment summaries, and related features.
- **News sources:** Your choices about which sources to include may be stored with your profile to personalize content.
- **Notification preferences:** Settings such as whether you want notifications may be stored and honored when sending alerts.

### 2.3 Push notifications

If you opt in to push notifications, **Apple Push Notification service (APNs)** is used. We associate your account with an **APNs device token** (a technical identifier for your device/installation) so we can deliver notifications, for example when news may relate to symbols you watch.

You can turn off notifications for tinCK in your device settings at any time.

### 2.4 Subscriptions

If you purchase or manage a subscription through Apple, **Apple processes the payment** and provides subscription status that we may mirror on our servers (for example tier, status, product identifier, and renewal timing) so the app can unlock features consistently with your entitlements.

### 2.5 Content you save

Features such as **saved articles or bookmarks** are stored **on your device** (for example using local app storage). They are tied to your on-device profile or session so the app can show your saves correctly.

### 2.6 Service operations and technical data

Like most online services, our infrastructure may automatically receive **technical information** when you use the app—such as **IP address**, **approximate region**, **device or OS type**, **app version**, **timestamps**, and **diagnostic or security logs**—as needed to operate, secure, and improve the Service. Our primary backend and auth provider is **Supabase**; processing is subject to their infrastructure and your device’s network path.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service (news, company information, watchlists, and related features).
- Authenticate you, keep your session secure, and sync preferences across devices when you use an account.
- Send push notifications you have agreed to receive.
- Handle subscriptions and entitlement checks.
- Protect against abuse, fraud, or technical issues.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Sharing of information

We may share information only as needed to run the Service:

- **Infrastructure and auth:** With Supabase (database, authentication, and related APIs) and, where applicable, **Apple** (Sign in with Apple, App Store / subscriptions, APNs) and **Google** (Google sign-in).
- **Legal and safety:** When required by law, legal process, or to protect our users, the public, or the Service.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to data brokers.

## 5. Data storage and retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account or data subject to applicable law and technical constraints (for example, retained backups for a limited period).

## 6. Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your choices

- **Notifications:** Manage tinCK notifications in iOS **Settings → tinCK → Notifications** (or equivalent).
- **Sign-in providers:** You can control what Apple or Google shares according to those platforms’ settings.
- **Account data:** Contact us if you need access, correction, or deletion requests regarding personal information we hold.

## 8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not intended for children under the age where parental consent is required in your region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children.

## 9. International users
